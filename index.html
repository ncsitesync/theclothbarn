[ Our Story ]

Seventy years in,
still family-run.

The Cloth Barn, located in Goldsboro North Carolina is one of the oldest and best-known fabric retailers in eastern North Carolina. Established in 1956 and still owned and operated by its founding family, we have earned a reputation for offering the largest selections of fabric along with outstanding custom made drapery, bedding and upholstery items.

From making your fabric choice, to picking a drapery or bedding style, to finding the perfect hardware, to installation of the product, The Cloth Barn is your one stop shop for all that is needed to make your house a home.

We offer a great selection of gorgeous drapery, upholstery, foam, drapery hardware, blinds and shutters. With our on-site workroom and knowledgable staff, we can help you with any need for your home décor.
